Safety center

Work safely. Go home safely.

Quick access to the core safety topics used around facility maintenance, PIT equipment, docks, conveyors, balers, and recycling machinery.

SAFETY
FIRST
Before servicing equipmentStop the machine, follow the approved site-specific energy-control procedure, isolate every energy source, release stored energy, and verify isolation. Only trained and authorized employees may perform the work.
01

Lockout / Tagout

Control electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal, chemical, and stored energy before maintenance, cleaning, adjustment, or jam removal.

OSHA hazardous energy guidance ↗
02

PIT Safety

Only trained and evaluated operators may use powered industrial trucks. Complete the required inspection and remove unsafe equipment from service.

OSHA PIT guidance ↗
03

Dock Doors

Confirm trailer restraint, dock plate condition, door condition, clear travel paths, and pedestrian separation before loading or maintenance work.

OSHA loading guidance ↗
04

Balers and Conveyors

Never reach into, climb onto, bypass guards, or clear a jam from operating equipment. Apply the approved lockout/tagout procedure first.

OSHA 1910.147 ↗
05

Personal Protective Equipment

Use the PPE required by the job hazard assessment and site procedure. Inspect PPE before use and replace damaged equipment.

Follow Living Spaces site requirements
06

Report a Hazard

Stop work when conditions are unsafe. Secure the area when possible and notify the responsible supervisor or safety contact immediately.

Do not operate defective equipment
